# Affiliate payee details: what to collect from the client

Programmes pay commission only to the person or company named as payee. That name must match:
- the bank account holder,
- the tax form, and
- (for a company) the Commercial Registration (CR).

Collect these details from the client and type them straight into each programme's own form.
**Never put the real values in this repo, in chat or in email threads.** Keep them in the password manager.

## Whose name: company (JEC) or Fahad personally?

DOABNO is the client's business, so the right payee is **the company (JEC)**, once it has:
1. a Commercial Registration (being obtained now, also needed for noon payouts), and
2. a Saudi **business** bank account in the company's name.

Until both exist, the only working choice is **Fahad as an individual**, paid to his personal Saudi bank account.

Things to know before choosing:
- **Changing later is hard.** On Amazon, the payee name and the tax form belong to the account. Moving from a person to a company usually means a new tax interview and sometimes a new account. If the CR is close (days or weeks), wait for it and sign up as the company.
- **Use one choice everywhere.** Use the same payee on Amazon.sa, noon, ArabClicks and the others, so all income lands in one place for bookkeeping, VAT and zakat.
- **Get the accountant's view.** Ask the client's accountant whether affiliate income may be paid to Fahad personally before a CR exists.

## Amazon.sa Associates: payment form fields

The account must belong to the payee: sign up with the company email and the payee's own phone number. Amazon pays only to a bank account in Saudi Arabia, the UAE or Egypt.

| Field | Company (JEC) | Individual (Fahad) |
|---|---|---|
| Payee Name | Company legal name exactly as on the CR and the bank account | Fahad's full name exactly as on his ID and bank account |
| Address lines 1-3 | Registered company address (as on the CR or the national address) | Fahad's national address |
| City, Region | e.g. Riyadh, Riyadh Region | same |
| Country | Saudi Arabia | Saudi Arabia |
| Phone | Company contact number, +966 | Fahad's mobile, +966 |
| Main contact | "The payee listed above" if Fahad runs the account; otherwise "Someone else" and enter that person | "The payee listed above" |
| U.S. person? | **No** (a Saudi company) | **No**, if Fahad is not a U.S. citizen or U.S. resident (green card or long stays in the U.S.). Confirm with him. |
| Tax interview that follows | W-8BEN-E (foreign company) | W-8BEN (foreign individual) |
| Bank details (next step) | Company bank name, IBAN (SA…), account holder = company name | Fahad's bank name, IBAN, account holder = Fahad |

Ask the client for:
- [ ] Decision: company or individual
- [ ] Legal name exactly as on the CR (company) or the ID (individual)
- [ ] National address (building no., street, district, city, postal code, additional no.)
- [ ] Contact phone (+966)
- [ ] U.S. person: yes or no
- [ ] Bank name and IBAN. **Fahad types these into Amazon himself.** We never see or store them.
- [ ] Company only: CR number and VAT number (for the tax interview and invoices)
